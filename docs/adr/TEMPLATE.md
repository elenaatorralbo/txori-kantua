# NNNN — Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Proposed on:** DD/MM/YYYY
- **Owner:** Name — [account](https://github.com/account)
- **Issue:** #N
- **Accepted on:** — (date and participants when approved)

## Context

What problem forces a decision now, what constraints exist (hardware,
schedule, privacy, team capacity) and what is known for certain versus what is
being assumed.

## Decision

What is decided, in one sentence. Then, the essential detail to implement it
without reopening the debate.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | For | Against | Why it is not chosen now |
|---|---|---|---|

## Consequences

- What becomes simpler.
- What becomes more complicated or limited.
- What additional work it creates and in which issue.

## Review criteria

Measurable conditions that would force this decision to be reopened. If a
threshold cannot be written, the decision is not concrete enough.

## Evidence

Links to measurements, tests or minutes that support the acceptance. While
the status is `Proposed`, this section states which evidence is missing and
who produces it.
